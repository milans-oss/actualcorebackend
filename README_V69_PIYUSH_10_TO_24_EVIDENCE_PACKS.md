## Evidence protocol

- Official annual or impact-report PDFs were actively searched before website evidence was compiled.
- Accessible substantive reports were read, including Seva Bharathi 2016–18 and CKSK 2020–21.
- Official annual-report pages were reviewed for Shri Mahaveer School, SportzVillage and Annapoorna Trust; source-ownership limitations are stated inside each pack.
- Samarthanam's 2024–25 PDF was located but could not be reliably parsed, so report-specific claims were not invented.
- No scores, rankings, recommended scores, ceilings or final decisions are inserted.
- Development Environment credits only demonstrated opportunities such as external competition, public presentation, child leadership, applied responsibility, educational visits, civic participation, specialist mentors and varied arts/sports pathways.
- Food, shelter, transport, healthcare, therapy, counselling, ordinary schooling and generic holistic-development language do not count by themselves.
- Existing PM responses and admin-added evidence remain preserved by the versioned migration logic.
