## Evidence protocol

- Official annual or impact-report PDFs were actively searched for before website evidence was compiled.
- Official PDFs were opened and read when accessible.
- Where a report was listed but inaccessible, outdated, mismatched or not found, the limitation is stated inside the evidence pack.
- Official programme, outcome, activity and success-story pages were used alongside report evidence.
- No PM scores, rankings, recommended scores, ceilings or decision rationales are inserted.
- Child Progression, Learning Model and Development Environment remain separate.
- Development Environment only credits demonstrated, recurring opportunities such as child leadership, public platforms, competitions, arts or sport progression, external mentors, educational exposure, applied responsibility and child-led civic or community activity.
- Food, shelter, transport, healthcare, therapy, counselling, ordinary schooling and generic “holistic development” language are not counted as Development Environment evidence by themselves.
- Existing PM responses and admin-added evidence are preserved by the versioned migration logic.
