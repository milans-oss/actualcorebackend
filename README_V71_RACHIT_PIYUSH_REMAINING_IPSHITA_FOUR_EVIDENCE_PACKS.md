## Research and evidence rules

- The official website and official annual/impact-report PDF were searched separately for every organisation.
- An available substantive official PDF was read where it could be retrieved reliably.
- When no report was located, was inaccessible, was historical, or was not programme-specific, the evidence pack states that limitation.
- Organisation-reported claims remain labelled and are not converted into independently verified outcomes.
- Programme, campus, age-group and national-network cohorts are kept separate.
- Development Environment only includes demonstrated opportunities such as sustained arts or sport, competitions, public platforms, leadership, civic participation, educational exposure, practical responsibility or market-facing work.
- Food, shelter, residence, healthcare, therapy, counselling and ordinary schooling are not counted as Development Environment evidence.

## Data safety

The migration continues to preserve PM-entered metric scores, reasons and responses. Presets contain only evidence text and source links.
