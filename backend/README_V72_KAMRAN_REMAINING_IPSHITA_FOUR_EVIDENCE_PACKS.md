## Evidence controls

- Official annual or impact reports were searched separately from programme pages.
- Inaccessible, historical, national-level, prospective and cross-state evidence is labelled explicitly.
- No score, recommended rank, ceiling or overall decision is pre-populated.
- Development Environment excludes baseline food, shelter, hostel, healthcare, therapy, counselling and ordinary schooling. It only records demonstrated opportunities such as competition, arts, sport, leadership, public presentation, educational exposure, practical responsibility, mentoring and civic participation.
- Evidence from different programmes, states, ages and beneficiary cohorts is not merged.
