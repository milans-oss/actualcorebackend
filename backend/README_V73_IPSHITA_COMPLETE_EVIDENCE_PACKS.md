## Evidence controls

- Official annual or impact reports were searched separately from official programme pages for every organisation.
- Report-backed evidence is used where a substantive official report could be located and read.
- Missing, inaccessible, historical, national-level, multi-state, institution-specific and promotional evidence is labelled explicitly.
- No score, recommended rank, ceiling or overall decision is pre-populated.
- Development Environment excludes food, shelter, hostel care, healthcare, therapy, counselling, ordinary schooling and infrastructure by themselves. It records only demonstrated opportunities such as arts, sport, competitions, public expression, child participation, leadership, educational exposure, professional interaction and applied projects.
- Evidence from distinct identities and cohorts is kept separate, especially:
  - Don Bosco Gulbarga versus BOSCO Bengaluru and Don Bosco Davangere.
  - Sevalaya Charitable Trust in Bengaluru at sevalayaindia.org versus the unrelated Chennai-based Sevalaya at sevalaya.org.
  - CRY's national results versus its specific open-school campaign.
  - IndiVillage's multi-location results versus Karnataka-specific delivery.
  - Individual Vivekananda Vidyavardhaka Sangha institutions versus the full network.
