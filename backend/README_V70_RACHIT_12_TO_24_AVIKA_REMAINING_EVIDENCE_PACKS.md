## Evidence method

Every pack contains only:

- Child Progression & Alumni Outcomes
- Learning Model
- Development Environment
- Source links and report-status cautions

Annual-report PDFs were prioritised. Where an NGO-specific report was unavailable, inaccessible, outdated or belonged to a partner, the pack states this explicitly. Partner evidence and national network evidence are not silently attributed to the assigned Karnataka child cohort.

Development Environment credits demonstrated opportunities such as student leadership, civic participation, public expression, competitions, educational visits, external mentors, arts or sport progression and applied responsibility. Food, shelter, hostel care, transport, healthcare, therapy, counselling, ordinary schooling and generic “holistic development” language are not counted by themselves.
