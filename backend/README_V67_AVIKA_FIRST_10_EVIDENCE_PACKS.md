## Evidence protocol

- Official annual or impact-report PDFs were actively searched for and read when available.
- Official website programme and activity pages were used alongside reports.
- No PM rankings, metric scores, recommended scores or ceilings are inserted.
- Child Progression, Learning Model and Development Environment are kept separate.
- Development Environment only credits demonstrated recurring opportunities such as leadership, public platforms, competitions, arts/sport pathways, mentors, educational exposure and child-led projects.
- Food, shelter, transport, healthcare, therapy, counselling, basic schooling and generic “holistic” language are not treated as Development Environment evidence by themselves.
- Existing PM responses and admin evidence are preserved by the workstream migration logic.
